Connect your phone to the laptop
  1. Connect the phone to this laptop with USB and enable USB debugging.
  2. In PowerShell, run adb reverse tcp:5001 tcp:5001.
  3. Open Chrome on the phone and go to http://localhost:5001/vr-test.html.
  4. Tap Enable phone motion, then Headset mode. Turn the phone sideways and place it in the viewer.

Motion sensors require a secure browser context. The localhost/ADB method avoids exposing the development server to your Wi-Fi network. You can also use an HTTPS-hosted copy.

Prototype only: no lens distortion correction, IPD/headset calibration, positional tracking, route data, or rover controls yet.
