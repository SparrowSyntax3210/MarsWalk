What these numbers mean

Route shape, terrain metrics, and route-cell classes come from the A* terrain planner. Time = distance ÷ entered average speed ÷ entered drive hours per sol. Energy = distance × entered energy use per km. Crew oxygen uses the editable per-person-per-sol assumption. These are planning estimates, not rover telemetry or certified life-support predictions. Radiation, slip, power variation, and detailed day/night operations are not modeled.

Audit a custom traverse

Paste a GeoJSON LineString or a list of [longitude, latitude] points. We sample up to 20 points from the global MDEM point service, then report sampled slope and local elevation variation. This is a coarse screening aid, not a high-resolution DTM hazard audit.
